## Introduction
This project explores the implementation of a Vedic Multiplier as a custom instruction in a RISC-V processor.

## Architecture
The design leverages the Urdhva Tiryakbhyam sutra for high-speed multiplication.

## Results
We observed a significant reduction in cycle count compared to standard multiplication algorithms on the CV32E40X core.
